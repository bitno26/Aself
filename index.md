# سجل متابعة الحضور

**Aself** - A web application for attendance tracking that works offline after the first load and can be installed on your phone and computer.

## Features

✅ **Works Offline** - The app functions completely offline after the initial load  
✅ **Installable** - Install on your phone or computer like a native app  
✅ **Private Data** - All records are saved only in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.  
✅ **Simple & Easy** - Clean, intuitive interface for tracking attendance  

## How It Works

1. Open the app in your browser
2. Start tracking attendance records
3. All data stays on your device - completely private
4. Install as a PWA on mobile or desktop for app-like experience
